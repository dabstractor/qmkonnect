## User Persona (if applicable)

**Target User**: a user who (a) installs QMKonnect and watches the tray icon to
know whether it found their board, and (b) opens Settings or the seeded
`config.toml` to point QMKonnect at the right board among several. Pre-F13/F14
these users saw a boolean "Connected" that lied green for a VIA-only board, and
raw hex VID/PID fields with a misleading `0xfeed` seeded example.

**Use Case**: user plugs in a QMK board they *haven't* flashed qmk_notifier onto.
The tray now reads `⚠ QMK board found — no qmk_notifier module (flash it)` (not a
false "Connected") → they follow the link and flash qmk_notifier. With two boards,
they open Settings, see the discovered-device list (`✓ Dactyl 0xFEED:0x0000 ←
qmk_notifier`, `✗ Keychron 0x3434:0x0123 ← QMK board, no module`), pick the
Dactyl, and QMKonnect writes its VID/PID for them — they never type hex.

**Pain Points Addressed**: (1) the false-green "Connected" for a VIA/Vial-only
board that silently does nothing; (2) the historical misread that `0xFEED` is
QMKonnect's default VID (it's a matching-dead crate fallback; `None` = wildcard);
(3) having to find/type raw VID/PID hex when the picker can self-populate and
write them.
